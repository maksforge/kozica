Kozica relief data

relief.pmtiles (a height grid) and contours.pmtiles (contour lines traced from
it) were built by scripts/build-relief.py from these terrain models, each
resampled, merged and converted by Kozica:

- Poland: Numeryczny Model Terenu, source: Główny Urząd Geodezji i Kartografii
  (GUGiK), https://www.geoportal.gov.pl/pl/dane/numeryczny-model-terenu-nmt/
- Slovakia: DMR 5.0, source: ÚGKK SR, licensed under Creative Commons
  Attribution 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
  Heights converted with ÚGKK SR's ETRS89 to Baltic 1957 grid, also CC BY 4.0.
- Czechia: DMR 4G, source: ČÚZK, licensed under Creative Commons Attribution
  4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The heights in trails.network, attached beside it with the map data, are
sampled from relief.pmtiles by scripts/build-network.py and come from the same
sources.

This notice covers the relief data only. The app's source code is not covered
by it.
